Privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026

PiRate does not collect your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

This policy covers the PiRate currency converter app for iPhone and Android, and this website. "We" means the independent developer of PiRate.

What stays on your phone

PiRate saves the following on your device only. It is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • your chosen currencies, amounts and settings
  • your trip budget and the spending you log
  • your rate alerts
  • the most recent exchange rates and chart history, so the app works offline

Deleting the app deletes this information. If you back up your phone with Apple or Google, the backup may include it, under your own account with them.

What the app sends over the internet

To fetch exchange rates, PiRate contacts the Frankfurter rates service. The request contains only currency codes and dates. As with any internet request, that service can see your device's IP address. PiRate sends no name, identifier, location or usage information, and makes no other network requests.

Your region

When you first open PiRate it reads your phone's region setting to suggest a starting currency. This happens on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Notifications

If you set a rate alert, PiRate asks permission to show notifications. Alerts are created on your phone itself. We do not run a notification server and receive no notification token.

Tips

Optional tips are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never see your name, card or payment details. We receive only the sales totals those stores report to developers.

Sharing

If you choose to share a result as an image, PiRate hands the image to the app you pick. Nothing is shared unless you ask.

Contacting us

If you email us, we see your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply, and delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

This website

This site uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

PiRate is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR and South Africa's POPIA give you rights to see, correct and delete personal information held about you. Because PiRate holds none, there is nothing for us to provide or delete, other than any emails you have sent us. Contact us and we will remove those on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If the app ever starts handling personal information, we will update this policy before that version is released.

PiRate is an independent app. Rates are daily reference rates for information only, not financial advice.
