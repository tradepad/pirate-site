Getting started

PiRate opens in your phone's own currency, so in South Africa it starts with the rand and in the UK with the pound. There's nothing to sign up for.

Two currencies matter most:

  • Home: your own money. It's the top row on the Convert tab.
  • Away: where you're spending or sending money. It's the second row.

Every tab uses the same home and away pair. Change it in one place and Rates, True cost and Trip follow.

The tabs along the bottom are Convert, Calc, Rates, True cost and Trip.

Convert

One list of currencies, all converted at once.

  • Type an amount: tap the number next to any currency. A keypad slides up and every other row updates as you type. Tap Done to put the keypad away.
  • Add a currency: tap Add currency below the list and search by currency or country. "Dubai", "Bali", "Kenya" and "UK" all work. There are over 150 currencies.
  • Reorder: hold a row, then drag it. Drag a currency to the top to make it your home currency, or to second place to make it your away currency.
  • Delete: swipe a row left or right. Changed your mind? Tap Undo.
  • World clock: each row shows the local time in a city that uses that currency. Where a currency covers several time zones, such as the US dollar or the euro, tap the time to pick your city.

The dot at the top shows how fresh the rates are. Tap it for details or to refresh.

Calc

A calculator that knows your currencies. Add up a bill, split it or take off a discount, and see the answer in your own money as you type.

  • Calc works in your away currency to start with. Tap the currency at the top of the display to calculate in another one.
  • Use + − × ÷ as on any calculator. × and ÷ are worked out before + and −.
  • Under the answer you'll see it converted into your home and away currencies.
  • ⌫ deletes one key; hold it, or tap AC, to start again.
  • Tap Convert this to send the answer to the Convert tab and see it in all your currencies.

Percentages work the way they do at the till:

  • 200 − 10% = 180 (10% off)
  • 80 + 15% = 92 (adding a 15% tip)
  • 50 × 10% = 5 (10% of 50)

Splitting a bill? Type the total, then ÷ 4 for four people.

Rates & alerts

See how a rate has moved and get a nudge when it reaches your target.

  • Chart: choose a week, month, year or five years (1W, 1M, 1Y, 5Y). You'll see the high and low for that period. Tap either currency to change it, or use the swap button to flip the pair.
  • Usual swing: the shaded fan shows how far this rate typically moves over the next 1, 3, 5 and 7 days, based on the last three months. It shows the size of a normal move, not which way the rate will go. Nobody can predict that.
  • Alerts: tap + New alert, enter the rate you're waiting for and tap Save alert. PiRate tells you when it's reached. Turn alerts on or off with the switch next to each one.

Alerts are checked each time you open PiRate, and every 30 minutes while it's open. They aren't checked while the app is closed, so open it now and then. Allow notifications for PiRate in your phone's settings.

True cost: spot the pirates

Banks and exchange desks rarely charge a fee you can see. Instead they give you a worse rate than the real one. This tab shows how much that costs you.

  • Under You change, type how much you're changing. Real rate gives shows what you'd get at the true mid-market rate.
  • Ranked lists your bank card, an exchange counter and an airport kiosk by how much you'd actually receive. Number 1 is the best deal.
  • The pie shows the slice the biggest pirate takes, and how much more you'd keep by choosing number 1.
  • Set your markups: each option starts with a typical markup: about 3% for a bank card, 5% for an exchange counter and 8% for an airport kiosk. Change these to match your own bank, and add any fixed fee. You can also switch on a travel card or money app to compare.
  • Use the share button to send the comparison to friends before they travel.

Rate not great today? Tap Set an alert to wait for a better one.

Trip

A holiday budget that keeps working with no signal.

  • Start a trip: give it a name, check the local and home currencies, enter your budget in your home currency and the number of days, then tap Start trip.
  • Treasure chest: shows what you've spent so far, how much budget is left and about how much you can spend each day.
  • Quick price check: common local prices already converted for you. Tap one to log it.
  • Log a spend: tap + Add spend, say what it was, enter the amount in local money and choose Card or Cash. PiRate adds what your card or exchange counter takes (from your True cost markups), so your total is the real one.
  • To delete a spend, hold it. When you're home, tap End trip to clear the log.

Settings

Tap the gold heart at the top of the Convert tab to:

  • Set your home and away currencies.
  • Read where the rates come from.
  • Rate PiRate on Google Play or share it with a friend ("Spread the word"). Every feature is free for everyone, forever.

About the rates

PiRate uses official reference rates from central banks, including the European Central Bank, through the free Frankfurter service. They're mid-market rates, before anyone adds a margin. That's why your bank's rate is almost always worse.

The latest rates are saved on your phone, so converting, the calculator and Trip all work offline. Charts you've already looked at are saved too.

PiRate is for information only. Rates are indicative and aren't financial advice.
