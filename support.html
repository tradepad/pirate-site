New to PiRate? The user guide explains every tab.

Where do the rates come from?

PiRate uses official reference rates from central banks, including the European Central Bank, supplied through the free Frankfurter service. Each bank publishes once or a few times a day, so the rates are updated during the day. They aren't live market prices.

Why is my bank's rate different?

Reference rates are the mid-market rate, before anyone adds a margin or fee. Banks, card companies and exchange kiosks add their own cut on top. The True cost screen is there to show you how big that cut is.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. PiRate saves the latest rates on your phone and uses them when you are offline. The dot at the top of the Convert screen tells you how fresh they are.

Why didn't my rate alert go off?

PiRate checks your alerts each time you open the app, and while it stays open. It does not check in the background, so open the app to see whether your target has been reached. Notifications also need to be allowed in your phone's settings.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads, subscriptions or locked features. Tips are optional, go to the developer, and unlock nothing.

I left a tip by mistake. Can I get a refund?

Tips are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds go through them: reportaproblem.apple.com for iPhone, or your order history in Google Play for Android. Email us if you get stuck.

How do I delete my data?

Everything PiRate stores is on your phone. Deleting the app removes it. See the privacy policy for details.
